# PRAYZVIBES — DESIGN AND LABEL-READINESS GATE

This is an internal, simulated creative-director and indie-label review. It is not an endorsement by an external label.

## Decision

Ready for artist review as a local draft. Not yet authorized for publication.

## Creative direction

- **Identity in three seconds — pass.** The opening names PRAYZVIBES immediately, shows the artist and black guitar, and establishes the line “Songs from Bavaria. Made to travel.”
- **Distinctive visual world — pass.** The design uses the existing olive, gold, cream and deep-brown DNA, real artist imagery and the established heavy display type instead of a generic music template.
- **Lyrical depth without over-explaining — pass.** The homepage moves through perspective, memory, courage, release, landscape and encounter while keeping each section to one principal idea.
- **Desktop and mobile authority — pass after correction.** Base type is 18 px on desktop and 16.5 px on mobile. Long German and French display lines were resized at narrow breakpoints without reducing the overall impact.

## Label and industry view

- **Current release is unambiguous — pass.** Transience is “out now” and accessible in one action; all four tracks are visible.
- **Artist proposition is clear — pass.** Independent songwriter, producer and live artist from Franconian Switzerland; street-born organic indie folk; solo acoustic format.
- **Live proposition is bookable — pass.** Format, settings and direct booking route are visible without inflated claims.
- **EPK route is immediate — pass.** Biography, release information, real coverage, assets and contacts remain available in all three languages.
- **Credibility — pass.** Existing press links are retained and no achievements, quotes, dates or partnerships were invented.
- **Fan path — pass.** Listening, films, playlists, physical editions, shop, support and newsletter paths remain available without competing with the music.

## Technical gate

- 33 HTML pages and 1,077 local references validated.
- English, German and French homepages checked at 320, 360, 390, 768, 1,100 and 1,440 px.
- Core About, Live, EPK and Listen pages checked on desktop and mobile in all three languages.
- No horizontal page overflow, missing local files, duplicate IDs, JavaScript syntax errors or browser console errors remain.
- Three local editorial films load from the site package.
- The former scheduled pre-release workflow was removed from this draft so it cannot overwrite the permanent release state later.
- The previously requested third-party name remains absent from the website.

## Final boundary

The draft remains local. The publishing repository is untouched until the artist explicitly approves this direction.
