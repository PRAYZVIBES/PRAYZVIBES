About
PRAYZVIBES

Independent songwriter, producer and live artist from Franconian Switzerland.

Independent by nature

An alias—not a hiding place.

PRAYZVIBES is an independent songwriter, producer and live artist from Franconian Switzerland in Bavaria, creating organic indie-folk that can expand into atmospheric, rhythmic and multilingual forms.

I write, perform and produce the songs independently. Acoustic guitar and voice are the centre; rhythm, electronics and other colours enter when the song asks for them.

PRAYZVIBES gives those different parts of the work one home. The name is the artist and the wider universe—PRAYZ remains the personal signature inside it.

How the songs take shape

Prepare the ground. Follow what moves.

A song may begin with a line, a rhythm, a memory or a question that refuses to leave. I bring craft, preparation and everything my senses have gathered—then leave room for instinct and the subconscious to speak.

The production grows around what is alive in the song instead of covering it. One song may stay close to voice and guitar; another opens toward percussion, electronic texture or a different language. Organic indie-folk is the root, not the ceiling.

From the songs

“My eyes hear sound.
My ears see silence.”

The songs are allowed to hold opposing truths: clarity and contradiction, resistance and surrender, stillness and movement. The tension is not something to solve too quickly. It is often where the song begins.

Live & open to the road
