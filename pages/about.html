How a song starts

Make space.
Let it move.

A line, a rhythm or a memory is usually enough. I follow it until the song feels like itself.

Some stay close to voice and guitar. Others ask for a little more air, pulse or colour.

From the songs

“My eyes hear sound.
My ears see silence.”

A good song can hold two truths at once.
