Meet PRAYZ

About
PRAYZVIBES

Singer-songwriter from Franconian Switzerland in Bavaria.

Where it began

A hillside.
A studio. The street.

As a teenager, PRAYZ was lying on a hillside listening to Bob Marley’s “Redemption Song” when music stopped feeling like background and became a compass.

The project later began in a beautiful studio beneath the rafters and found its shape in the streets, through encounters with people across cultures, generations and languages.

There was no formal route. Personal exploration led through different instruments and styles until the acoustic guitar became home.

How a song starts

Make space.
Let it move.

Most songs begin with a feeling, a question or an experience that refuses to leave. I follow it until the song feels honest enough to stand on its own.

Voice and acoustic guitar remain the core. Rhythm, texture and colour have to earn their place.

The centre

Honest voice.
Open meaning.

The songs begin with lived experience, but leave room for listeners to find their own perspective.

Live & on the road

One guitar.

Original songs and selected covers for intimate rooms, open air and places in between.

The name
