Artist biography

Street-shaped indie folk from Bavaria.

PRAYZVIBES is the independent, self-managed project of singer-songwriter PRAYZ, based in Franconian Switzerland in Bavaria.

As a teenager, PRAYZ experienced a formative moment while listening to Bob Marley’s “Redemption Song” on a hillside. Later, a beautiful studio beneath the rafters became the project’s first home; from there, the songs moved into the streets and real encounters.

Voice and acoustic guitar sit at the centre. PRAYZ writes, performs and produces the songs independently, adding rhythm and texture only when the material asks for them.

Around that acoustic centre sits a wider listening world: independent roots and soul, global street music and organic electronic rhythm, mapped through four public playlists curated by PRAYZVIBES.

Live, PRAYZVIBES is a flexible solo act with vocals, acoustic guitar and foot percussion.
