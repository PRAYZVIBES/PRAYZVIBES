Artist biography

Indie folk with room to breathe.

PRAYZVIBES is an independent singer-songwriter from Franconian Switzerland in Bavaria.

Voice and acoustic guitar sit at the centre. The songs are written, performed and produced independently, with rhythm and texture added only when the material asks for them.

Live, PRAYZVIBES is a flexible solo act with vocals, acoustic guitar and foot percussion.

Live profile

Songs that travel light.

Solo vocals, acoustic guitar and foot percussion. Sets mix original songs with selected covers and usually run for 1–2 hours; shorter formats are available by arrangement.

Suitable for festivals, intimate venues, private events and open-air settings.
