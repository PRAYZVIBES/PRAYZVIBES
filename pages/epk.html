Artist biography

Street-born indie folk from Franconian Switzerland.

PRAYZVIBES is an independent songwriter, producer and live artist from Franconian Switzerland in Bavaria.

As a teenager, PRAYZ heard Bob Marley’s Redemption Song during a hillside picnic with friends and felt a profound sense of peace he never forgot.

Years later, PRAYZVIBES began in a basement with only PRAYZ and a guitar. The work later grew into a professional practice with its own studio beneath the rafters, then moved into streets, small rooms and stages.

Self-taught, PRAYZ explored different instruments and styles until the acoustic guitar felt like home. Voice and black acoustic guitar remain at the centre; he writes, performs and produces independently, adding rhythm and texture only when a song asks for them.

The name joins praise and vibes: gratitude in the voice, movement in the music, with a quiet echo of prayer that is present but never prescribed.

Live, PRAYZVIBES is an intimate solo act built around voice, acoustic guitar and foot percussion, adaptable to the room.
