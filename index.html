First coin · Nuremberg · during Bardentreffen 2023

After the last note, she came back.

A young couple had stopped while PRAYZ played near Nuremberg Castle. He was not collecting money. The coin she gave him became the first money he earned through music. He still keeps it.

He no longer remembers which song he was playing. He remembers the moment.

Hillside · attic · street

Music stopped being background.

PRAYZVIBES began with a formative teenage moment on a hillside, listening to Bob Marley’s Redemption Song — and music stopped being background. Later, songs took shape in a small wooden attic and in the street. Today, PRAYZVIBES writes, records, produces and performs independently in Franconian Switzerland.

The songs begin with voice and a black acoustic guitar; rhythm, texture and silence enter when the music asks for them.
