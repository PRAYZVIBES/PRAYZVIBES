Hillside · atelier · street

I followed the songs into the open.

When I heard Bob Marley’s Redemption Song on a hillside as a teenager, music stopped being background. Later, I began shaping songs in a beautiful atelier beneath the rafters and carrying them into the street. That line—from stillness to contact—still runs through PRAYZVIBES.

The songs begin with voice and a black acoustic guitar. Rhythm, texture and silence enter when the music asks for them.

First response · Nuremberg · 2023

I forgot the song, not the moment.

During Bardentreffen in 2023, near Nuremberg Castle, a young couple stopped while I played. I was not collecting money. After the last note, the woman came back and gave me a coin—the first money I ever earned through music. I still keep it.
